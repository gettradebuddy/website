About TradeBuddy

A simple social platform designed to help traders discover and connect with other traders who trade like them.

Our purpose

TradeBuddy helps traders build meaningful connections based on the trading preferences they choose to share.

Members can discover compatible traders, build a network, message privately, share charts, and participate in a public trading Feed.

What TradeBuddy is not

TradeBuddy is not a broker, investment adviser, signal service, copy-trading platform, or financial institution. TradeBuddy does not execute trades or guarantee trading outcomes.

Our approach

Keep it simple. Launch first. Improve with real user feedback.
