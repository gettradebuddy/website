FAQ

Simple answers.

Is TradeBuddy a broker?

No. TradeBuddy does not execute trades, hold funds, or provide brokerage services.

Does TradeBuddy provide trading signals?

No. TradeBuddy is a networking platform. Trading decisions remain entirely your responsibility.

How are traders recommended?

TradeBuddy uses the profile information and trading preferences you choose to provide to recommend potentially relevant traders. Recommendations are informational and do not guarantee compatibility or results.

Is TradeBuddy free?

Yes. A Free Membership is available. Premium adds unlimited new conversations and Nearby Traders.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. You can permanently delete your account from Settings. You can also use our public account-deletion page if you cannot access the app.
