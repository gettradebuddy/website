Delete Your TradeBuddy Account

You can permanently delete your account from inside the TradeBuddy app through Settings → Delete Account.

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete inside the app

Open TradeBuddy and go to Settings → Delete Account. Follow the verification steps to permanently delete your account.

Cannot access the app?

Submit the email address used for your TradeBuddy account. For privacy, this page will not confirm whether an account exists.

What is deleted

  • Profile and onboarding information
  • Trading preferences and location data
  • Connections and requests
  • Feed posts, comments, reactions, and uploaded Feed images
  • Notifications, preferences, and membership information

Messages

Existing direct-message history may remain visible to the other participant for conversation continuity. Your identity is replaced with “Deleted User,” profile access is removed, and no new messages can be sent.
